The Problem

Scheduling software is restricted on purpose — and that's exactly what breaks it.

Microsoft Project and Primavera P6 are deliberately locked to a trained planning function. An untrained edit to predecessor/successor logic can corrupt the critical path across an entire programme, so access stays narrow. The same design choice that protects the schedule also cuts it off from the people who see problems first — operators, test engineers, manufacturing supervisors — who have no access and no training on the tool.

The result is a structural lag, not a communication failure. It scales with how scattered the organisation is — not with how good the scheduling tool is.

Why Existing Tools Don't Close It

Enterprise software solves its own domain. Nobody reasons across them.

Primavera / MSP
Manages the schedule — assumes information already reached the planner.
ALICE
Optimises sequencing once the schedule is current — doesn't solve how it becomes current.
SAP / ERP
Records financial and procurement transactions in its own silo.
Power BI
Visualises whatever data it's given — after the fact.
Agile ceremonies
Adds stand-ups and tracking overhead on top of the same siloed departments.

Case Example

Automotive manufacturing programme — regulatory deadline

A programme tied to an EU emissions certification deadline risked delaying a much larger vehicle programme. The critical path ran through a custom-built instrument using a specialist material not yet supported by existing certification tooling, compounded by limited UK supply, fixed hot/cold-chamber test windows that couldn't be compressed, and a factory shutdown period. Governance met monthly — issues discovered between cycles waited for the next board unless someone manually flagged them as urgent.

Where Solvia Intervenes

Not another scheduling tool. The layer that gets reality into the one you already have.

01 — CAPTURE

Reads what teams already use

Photos of shift sheets, existing internal trackers, email — no new login, no training.

02 — MAP

Links signal to task

Identifies which work package or milestone the event actually affects.

03 — FORECAST

Models the impact

Projects the effect on dates and critical path, and generates recovery scenarios.

04 — ESCALATE

Flags by exception

Notifies senior stakeholders immediately when it matters — not at the next monthly cycle.

Case example

Automotive manufacturing programme — regulatory deadline

Illustrative scenario

A single test window nearly delayed a much larger vehicle programme

A programme tied to an EU emissions certification deadline risked delaying a much larger vehicle programme. The critical path ran through a custom-built instrument using a specialist material not yet supported by existing certification tooling — compounded by limited UK supply, fixed hot/cold-chamber test windows that couldn't be compressed, and a factory shutdown period. Governance met monthly. Issues discovered between cycles waited for the next board unless someone manually flagged them as urgent.

Composite scenario based on patterns from capital and manufacturing programmes. Not a named client engagement.
